# Founder Media Decisions — 2026-09-24

These decisions override earlier conservative lab holds where they conflict.

## Identity

- Do not publish or infer a real-world identity/name from atlas evidence.
- Use neutral opaque IDs such as `founder-main-01`.

## Hard rejects

- Media containing a child: reject from the product.
- Selected media containing another identifiable person/identity: reject from the default product library.

## Brand context

Allowed for product use when the brand is incidental/contextual, including major-brand environments or visible fashion/automotive branding.

Examples:
- GUCCI signage in a city scene;
- CHANEL-marked fashion context;
- Rolls-Royce as a contextual prop/background.

Rules:
- do not imply brand endorsement, partnership, sponsorship or affiliation;
- preserve ordinary editorial/contextual framing.

Specific branded campaign/product stills are reviewed case by case. They are not automatically released merely because a logo is visible.

## Editorial / exhibit / art content

Founder approved:
- editorial-shoot imagery;
- exhibit/gallery/art-context imagery;
for use in the Influencer OS product, subject to normal provenance and identity rules.

## Public GitHub publication is separate

Approval for product use is not automatically the same as permission to publish full-resolution/web derivatives into the public experiment repository.

The repository-publication decision remains a separate explicit gate.
