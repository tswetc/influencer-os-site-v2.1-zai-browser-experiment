# Design Set B — Cinematic Fashion

## Intent

Push Influencer OS toward a high-fashion campaign / cinematic editorial product while preserving serious creator tooling.

Primary influences:
- IVRESS — Spin a Tale: sequencing, texture, image choreography, pacing;
- Gionatan Nese: large photography, light/material presence, minimal UI;
- fashion/editorial restraint in the spirit of Acne Studios;
- rare, meaningful immersive moments rather than constant spectacle.

## Emphasis

- full-bleed media and deliberate chapter pacing on public surfaces;
- strong identity continuity across Character → Image → Video;
- motion used as narrative transition;
- texture/light/material as part of product atmosphere;
- studio UI appears when needed and recedes when media/evidence is primary.

## Avoid

- generic cinematic trailer site;
- permanent WebGL spectacle;
- hiding product controls for aesthetics;
- glossy AI gradients and glassmorphism;
- copying IVRESS compositions literally.

## Invariant

This brief changes art direction, not Influencer OS semantics.
