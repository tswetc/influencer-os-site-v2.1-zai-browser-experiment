# Readymag Curated Shortlist — 2026-09-24

## A. Jo Iijima

Live:
https://joiijima.com/

Readymag:
2025 Readymag Choice.

Useful role:
- primary visual anchor for controlled chaos;
- precise typography against bold colour/form;
- heterogeneous layers that still feel intentional;
- portfolio as a self-contained world.

Best for:
- experimental public site;
- unusual composition;
- bold type/colour;
- breaking generic SaaS symmetry.

Do not use as a workbench-UX reference.

## Selection principle

Readymag scouting can be broad.

Run assignment stays narrow:
- choose the exact project;
- assign one exact role;
- measure it;
- transfer the mechanic;
- do not average the whole Readymag aesthetic.
