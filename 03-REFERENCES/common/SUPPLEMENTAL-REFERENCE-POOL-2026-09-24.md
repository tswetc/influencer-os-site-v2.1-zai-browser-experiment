# Supplemental Reference Pool — 2026-09-24

These are **candidate** references found during current external research.

## Jo Iijima — current award signal

https://joiijima.com/

Readymag Websites of the Year 2025 selected Jo Iijima's portfolio as Readymag Choice and highlighted its layered worlds, balance of chaos/order, unpredictability and intentionality.

Why it may matter for IOS:
- evidence that strongly authorial heterogeneous composition can still feel deliberate;
- useful for breaking out of generic SaaS grids without abandoning hierarchy.

Already present in the Persona archive.

https://brand.ivress.co.jp/en/

Utsubo's FWA celebration states that IVRESS won FWA Site of the Month for May 2026 and describes it as a storytelling-driven site built with Three.js and WebGPU.

Why it may matter for IOS:

Already a primary reference.

## Matteo Courquin — FWA of the Day, 2026-09-24

Source index:
https://thefwa.net/

FWA describes the project as a no-scroll interactive portfolio / creative playground with alternative navigation, motion and 3D interactions within a single screen.

Why it may matter for IOS:

- should be evaluated carefully against product usability before adoption.

## Whorl — generative design tool

https://design.whorl.app/

FWA Wire described Whorl as a browser generative design tool where users randomise style/shape/colour/motion/layout and then shape results manually in real time.

Why it may matter for IOS:
- useful creator-tool reference;
- supports the principle “AI/generative exploration + visible manual control”;
- potential reference for parameter exploration without hiding authoring logic.

## A24 experimental filmography exploration

FWA case index:
https://thefwa.com/cases/a24

Described as an experimental non-commercial 3D disc-based experience for discovering a filmography.

Why it may matter for IOS:
- candidate for spatial model/example browsing;
- not suitable as a default studio interaction without usability proof.

## Research rule

Before promoting any candidate into an experiment:
- open the current live site;
- perform a teardown;
- identify exact mechanics;
- assign one specific design role;
- add a DO NOT COPY section;
- reject it if it does not translate to an Influencer OS user job.
