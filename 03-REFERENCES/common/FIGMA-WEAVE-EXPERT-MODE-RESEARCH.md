## Observed product mechanics

Current official Figma material describes Weave as a node-based canvas for generative workflows.

Observed useful mechanics:
- connect models/assets/transforms into visible pipelines;
- branch ideas;
- remix outputs;
- compare approaches;
- refine across models;
- save/reuse workflows;
- publish complex workflows as simpler tools;
- connect Figma frames into the workflow as nodes;
- expose selected design inputs while preserving the underlying design structure.
