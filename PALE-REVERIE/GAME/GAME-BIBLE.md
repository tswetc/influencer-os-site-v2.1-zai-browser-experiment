# PALE REVERIE — Game Bible

Status: CURRENT FOUNDER CANON

## Identity

**Working title:** PALE REVERIE  
**Full title:** PALE REVERIE: REQUIEM OF THE EBB

**Genre:** third-person 3D anime action-RPG with exploration, real-time elemental combat, surreal environmental storytelling and authored cinematic moments.

**Current platform target:** browser/WebGL first.

The quality reference is the polish/readability of a modern high-end anime action-RPG. Genshin Impact can be used as a structural reference for cel-shading, animation readability, camera/combat clarity and environmental polish, but PALE REVERIE must remain original: no copied characters, lore, maps, UI, props, effects, names or proprietary art.

## Creative promise

The player should feel that they are moving through a beautiful world that has already ended once but does not fully understand that it ended.

The game is:
- melancholic rather than grim;
- surreal rather than random;
- elegant rather than noisy;
- strange but coherent;
- nostalgic without fake paper/pencil/vintage textures;
- anime in character readability, but fashion-art / dream-film in composition.

A strong PALE REVERIE image should be recognizable even with the UI removed.

## World premise

The world is **Evertide**.

Above it hangs the pearl Moon **Mnemea**.

Tides carry memory. Memories that return can re-enter people, places and objects. Memories that fail to return become residue, spirits, distortions or physical phenomena.

The catastrophe called **The Great Ebb** stopped the normal return.

The sea became black reflective mirror in many places. Time and memory stopped flowing correctly. Parts of the world preserved impossible fragments of people, cities and events.

The player initially believes the disaster is something that happened to Eira's world.

The deeper truth is that **Eira is the Heart of the Moon** and the Great Ebb is connected to her attempt to stop Mnemea's hunger for memory.

## Main heroine

**Eira** is the primary playable heroine and the most important visual acceptance target.

Signature:
- pale cool porcelain / gray-blue skin;
- narrow elongated face;
- large half-lidded pale ice-blue anime eyes;
- small pale mouth;
- slightly pointed ears;
- tiny beauty mark;
- enormous radial lavender-pink mane forming a sunburst/halo silhouette;
- liquid-silver fitted high-collar suit with central zipper;
- lunar/pearl jewelry and blue stones;
- ear-cuff/headset detail;
- asymmetric earrings;
- pearl moon-sphere weapon;
- Carr, a glossy blue-black crow with amber eye.

Her silhouette must be unmistakable from a distance.

If the game is technically impressive but Eira does not resemble the founder references, the visual mission is not complete.

## Main cast

**Velm** — pale sculptural figure with ring/thorn/spiral crown, pale-blue eyes/crystal tears, mechanical-organic vines and a liquid-silver chakram. Solemn, ceremonial, guilty.

**Blu** — vivid cobalt hair, punk-pop styling, pink/white top, wide dark trousers, chains/charms, green lips, pointed boots and Dream-linked energy. Disruptive, alive, irreverent.

**Carr** — black/blue-black crow with amber eye. Warning, witness, navigation/story signal and emotional counterpoint.

**The Sleeper** — giant liquid-chrome head with transparent regions containing miniature worlds and impossible cheerful objects. Beautiful and uncanny, not gore.

**Warden of the Ebb** — bowed pale entity with long arms, chain/ring structures, huge perforated Lace membranes/wings and several glowing pearl cores.

## Seven regions

1. **Silver Shoals** — black mirror sea, pale rock, whale-rib arches, submerged amber lamps.
2. **Lavender Manes** — lavender hills, wind rivers, mane-like trees, glass sky-whales.
3. **Olive Light Swamp** — olive/turquoise fog, giant lily pads, lime lanterns, cream stone faces.
4. **Lace Cathedral** — black-violet whale-bone gothic, perforated Lace architecture, pearl light, giant organ.
5. **Clock Cliff / Orrery** — bronze and ivory, golden lines, frozen moments, astronomical machinery.
6. **Glass Garden** — saturated pop-blue dream-space, giant liquid-chrome head, miniature worlds, altered gravity.
7. **Sea of Silence** — inverted sky, teal-black space/water, lunar shards and upside-down Oriel.

Each region needs its own silhouette language, palette, traversal identity, memory phenomenon, enemy/ecology logic, landmark, audio atmosphere and narrative function.

## Traversal

Movement should be expressive enough that crossing the world is enjoyable.

Core:
- walk/run/sprint;
- responsive jump;
- dodge;
- climbing where authored;
- swimming where relevant;
- wind-current traversal;
- **Mirror Step** across black water with circular ripples;
- **Moon Glide**, visually connected to Eira's mane/lunar identity;
- sky-whale and region-specific traversal.

Camera is part of movement quality.

## Combat

Primary elemental language:
- Wind;
- Tide;
- Dream;
- Ebb;
- Silver.

Interactions/reactions must be tactically understandable and visually distinct.

Examples:
- Wind + Tide → vortex / fluid-air control;
- Tide + Dream → conductive dream discharge.

Combat priorities:
1. readable anticipation;
2. responsive input;
3. coherent animation transitions;
4. hit reaction / hit stop;
5. camera clarity;
6. VFX state communication;
7. spectacle after readability.

## Living world

Possible systems:
- time of day;
- weather;
- wind;
- reactive grass;
- reflective/reactive water;
- lantern states;
- fauna;
- NPC routines;
- environmental audio;
- atmospheric particles/events.

A few convincing reactions are better than dozens of shallow systems.

## Memory as gameplay

Memory is the central theme and must affect play.

Major situations should make memory change one or more of:
- NPC state;
- environment;
- traversable path;
- dialogue;
- encounter;
- reward;
- region state;
- final choice.

The game should force the player to question whether restoring every memory is actually good.

## Narrative destination

Eira seeks five Lunar Pearls while learning what the Great Ebb did to Evertide.

The apparent quest is restoration.

The deeper conflict becomes:
- restore the old cycle;
- preserve the stopped world;
- or create a third condition where memory can exist without Mnemea consuming it in the old way.

The project targets three meaningful endings.

## Visual contract

Required:
- clean dimensional anime 3D;
- cel shading;
- colored shadows;
- thin colored outlines;
- controlled anime facial planes;
- broad stylized hair masses plus silhouette detail;
- characteristic hair highlight band/ring;
- rim light where useful;
- pearl, chrome, liquid-silver and glass materials;
- selective bloom;
- volumetric light/fog;
- stylized clouds/sky;
- reflective stylized water;
- restrained particles.

Explicitly not the target:
- paper;
- pencil;
- graphite;
- watercolor;
- canvas;
- sketchbook;
- global grain/noise;
- generic low-poly primitives presented as final hero art.

## Color and mood

Core families:
- pale gray-blue;
- pearl white;
- liquid silver;
- lavender/pink;
- muted mint/sage/olive;
- teal-black;
- amber/gold accents;
- selective cobalt, lime or red-orange.

Pastel does not mean washed-out everywhere.
Dark fantasy does not mean every scene is black.

## Product priority order

1. Eira / hero identity and reference fidelity.
2. Movement, camera and animation feel.
3. Combat readability and responsiveness.
4. World art direction / authored density.
5. Story/progression and memory-system meaning.
6. Stability, save integrity and performance.
7. Optional side systems.

Do not add side features while a higher-priority red gate remains unresolved unless they directly help that gate.

## Verification philosophy

No false acceptance.

A worker may not call the game done because it builds, debug APIs can reach the ending, one vision score is high, triangle count is high, many features exist or one screenshot is attractive.

Evidence combines:
- matched reference/render comparisons;
- multi-angle character checks;
- pairwise old/new comparison;
- real input-level playthrough;
- fresh-save progression;
- deterministic tests where appropriate;
- browser/performance evidence;
- clean-room export verification;
- founder visual/taste review for final art acceptance.

Depth beats inflated breadth.
