# PALE REVERIE — Characters

## Eira

Role: primary heroine / player viewpoint / hidden Heart of Mnemea.

### Visual identity
- young adult;
- tall, slim, elongated proportions;
- pale gray-blue porcelain skin;
- narrow elongated face;
- half-lidded pale ice-blue anime eyes;
- small pale mouth;
- slightly pointed ears;
- tiny beauty mark;
- huge lavender-pink radial mane, visually closer to a sunburst/halo than ordinary long hair;
- moon-energy state can shift toward silver-white;
- glossy liquid-silver fitted high-collar suit;
- center zipper;
- amber-blue oval amulet;
- blue stones at wrists/ankles;
- silver pointed nails;
- metallic ear-cuff/headset with small amber indicator and thin wire;
- asymmetric earrings: pearl / red-orange drop;
- pearl moon-sphere weapon with four-point glint.

### Motion identity
Eira should feel light but not weightless.

Key qualities:
- restrained idle;
- long-limbed controlled run;
- mane lag/settle;
- decisive dodge;
- Mirror Step with circular memory-water response;
- Moon Glide expanding her silhouette.

### Character arc
The question changes from "What happened to this world?" to "What did I do to this world?" and finally "What am I willing to become so it can continue?"

## Carr

Glossy blue-black/black crow with amber eye.

Functions:
- companion;
- warning;
- visual framing;
- scout/navigation hint;
- witness to Eira's forgotten past;
- emotional contrast through small natural behavior.

Do not reduce Carr to a permanently attached shoulder prop.

## Velm

Role: ritual knowledge / guilt / order.

Visual:
- sculptural white body/skin;
- pale-blue eyes;
- ornate interlocking ring/thorn/spiral crown;
- pale blue crystal drops;
- beaded tears;
- mechanical-organic vines;
- liquid-silver chakram.

Presence: quiet, precise, burdened.

## Blu

Role: Dream / disruption / emotional motion.

Visual:
- long vivid cobalt-blue hair;
- pink-white striped top;
- wide black trousers;
- chain belt;
- heart/star/whale-like charms;
- metallic ear element;
- red glowing earring;
- green lips;
- dark nails;
- pointed brown boots.

Blu is allowed to be more pop, saturated and contemporary than the surrounding world.

Do not recolor Eira and call it Blu.

## The Sleeper

A giant liquid-chrome head with transparent regions and miniature worlds inside.

Possible interior motifs:
- tiny landscapes;
- candy-like snails;
- a goat in a heart-shaped window;
- fan-leaf trees;
- smiling star/sun;
- red ribbons.

Tone: beautiful, uncanny, dreamlike, not gore.

## Warden of the Ebb

Boss/guardian figure.

Visual:
- bowed pale body;
- long arms;
- chains/rings emerging from torso;
- huge perforated Lace membranes/wings;
- 3–4 glowing pearl cores.

Pearl cores naturally communicate combat state/weak points.

The Lace membranes should change silhouette across phases without hiding the player.
