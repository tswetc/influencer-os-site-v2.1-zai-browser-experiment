# PALE REVERIE — World & Lore

## Evertide

Evertide is a world in which memory participates in physical reality.

The tide is a carrier of memory. People, places and objects can shed impressions into it. Returning tide can restore fragments; failed return can distort them.

This rule should remain legible in visual storytelling:
- flooded rooms remember inhabitants;
- reflections show prior states;
- a place can physically retain an event;
- voices may belong to memory-residue rather than a living NPC;
- architecture can be grown from repeated remembrance.

## Mnemea

Mnemea is the pearl Moon.

It is associated with tidal motion, memory circulation, pearl/lunar energy, hunger/consumption and Eira's hidden origin.

The Moon should feel like a force before it is fully explained.

## The Great Ebb

The Great Ebb interrupted the normal cycle.

Consequences:
- sea surfaces freeze into black mirror states;
- memory ceases to return correctly;
- certain moments remain preserved;
- spirits and Lace form from unreturned memory;
- Tidekeepers sleep or become dormant;
- regions drift into different kinds of temporal/mnemonic failure.

## Lace

Lace is a family of organic/perforated memory structures and entities.

It can appear as translucent membranes, architecture, windows, wings and biological filigree.

It is not generic gothic decoration. It represents memory that has grown structure without fully becoming life.

## Tidekeepers

Ancient chrome/pearl entities connected to management or regulation of tidal memory.

Their history should be revealed gradually.

They should feel technological, ceremonial and organic at once.

## Eira's hidden truth

Eira is the Heart of the Moon.

The Great Ebb is connected to her act of stopping or interrupting Mnemea's old hunger/cycle.

This revelation should reframe earlier scenes instead of simply adding a twist.

## Central thematic question

What makes a person real?

Possible answers include continuity of memory, present consciousness, relationships/recognition, choice, embodiment and willingness to let go.

The three endings may embody competing answers.

## Ending directions

### Return
Restart the old tide/memory cycle.

Benefit: restoration and continuity.  
Cost: Mnemea's old hunger/cycle may return.

### Stillness
Preserve the stopped/reverie state.

Benefit: protected memories and preserved selves remain.  
Cost: the world cannot fully grow/change.

### New Tide
Eira transforms or gives up the old Heart function to create a new memory circulation.

Benefit: memory can move without simply returning to the old system.  
Cost: Eira may cease to exist in her current form or lose a central part of herself.

These are narrative directions, not permission for a simplistic last-minute three-button choice.
