# PALE REVERIE — Visual Direction

## Primary target

High-quality stylized 3D anime rendering with surreal fashion-fantasy art direction.

The image should feel authored and cinematic, not like a generic procedural Three.js demo.

## Character rendering

- clean cel-shaded value grouping;
- colored shadows;
- thin colored outlines;
- restrained face topology;
- crisp eyes/iris/catchlight;
- matte skin relative to suit/chrome;
- broad readable hair clumps plus secondary silhouette detail;
- signature hair highlight band/ring;
- rim light as compositional tool;
- no plastic toy appearance.

## Materials

### Liquid silver / chrome
Strong environment response, clean specular shapes, controlled highlights and enough roughness variation to avoid mirror-ball look.

### Pearl
Soft iridescence, not rainbow hologram everywhere.

### Glass
Readable transmission/reflection without destroying scene legibility.

### Black mirror water
Should read as water/reverie surface, not a flat black plane.

Use reflection, ripple response, depth/fog cues, local disturbance and controlled roughness.

## Lighting

Use motivated cinematic light: moon, sky, submerged lamps, portals/pearls and region-specific emissive organisms.

Volumetric light and bloom are accents, not camouflage for weak geometry/materials.

## Atmosphere

Volumetric fog, distant color compression, layered silhouettes, moving particles, occasional rain/mist/water spray, god rays and wind-visible vegetation where justified.

## Camera

Gameplay:
- stable spatial comprehension;
- strong character framing;
- speed-aware FOV only when useful;
- avoid constant aggressive motion.

Cinematics:
- orbit;
- crane/dolly;
- FPV-like pursuit;
- macro reveal;
- reflection/match-cut transitions;
- light-source-based lens flare;
- foreground occlusion/parallax.

## Effects

Effects should have physical/visual logic:
- rain impacts;
- surface ripples;
- wetness;
- refracted light;
- wind deformation;
- motes interacting with movement;
- hit sparks;
- fluid/silver response;
- dream conductivity.

Avoid VFX soup.

## Color

Core: silver / pearl / cool porcelain / lavender / mint / olive / teal-black.

Accents: amber / gold / cobalt / lime / red-orange.

## Explicit exclusions

Do not introduce as the global game style:
- paper texture;
- pencil;
- graphite;
- watercolor;
- canvas;
- charcoal;
- sketch hatching;
- print grain;
- notebook aesthetic.

Nostalgia should come from composition, color, music, memory and cinematic behavior—not fake paper.
