# PALE REVERIE — Narrative Structure

This defines the dramatic spine, not final dialogue.

## Act I — The world after the tide

Eira wakes into Silver Shoals with incomplete context.

Player learns:
- black mirror water;
- Carr;
- basic movement/combat;
- memory is physically present.

Initial motivation: find the Lunar Pearls / understand the Ebb.

The world treats Eira as both stranger and something half-recognized.

## Act II — Memory is not truth

Lavender Manes and/or Olive Light expose contradictions:
- two memories of the same event;
- NPCs changed by what was returned;
- restoration can harm as well as heal.

The player learns memory can be edited by conditions of return.

## Act III — The keepers

Velm / Tidekeeper material reveals there was a system regulating memory circulation.

Eira's relation to that system becomes suspicious.

The Lace Cathedral turns remembrance into architecture/music.

The Warden should feel like a guardian protecting a terrible rule, not an arbitrary monster.

## Act IV — Dream / Blu / the false self

Glass Garden explores whether a coherent present self matters more than accurate memory.

Blu challenges solemn restoration logic.

The Sleeper can contain contradictory miniature worlds, each internally true.

## Act V — Time and causality

Clock Cliff/Orrery confronts the player with preserved moments.

The question becomes: if a moment never advances, is preserving it love or refusal?

## Act VI — Sea of Silence

The player reaches memories with no surviving owner and the inverted city Oriel.

The distinction between world and Moon begins to collapse.

Eira discovers she is not simply a person chosen by Mnemea.

She is tied to the Moon's Heart/function.

## Act VII — The Heart of the Moon

The Great Ebb is reinterpreted as an act connected to Eira stopping the old hunger/cycle.

The final conflict is not "kill evil moon".

It is deciding what relationship memory, loss and identity should have after the old system fails.

## Endings

Three major ending families:
- Return;
- Stillness;
- New Tide.

Earlier choices should influence available dialogue/state/consequence.

The final decision should feel earned through repeated gameplay examples of the same philosophical conflict.

## Narrative implementation rule

Every major lore reveal should ideally have at least two of:
- environment evidence;
- gameplay consequence;
- character dialogue;
- cinematic;
- earlier foreshadowing.

Avoid lore documents as the only delivery mechanism.
